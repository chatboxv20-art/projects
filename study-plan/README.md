## 1. Faculty & Program Overview

The Faculty of Business (كلية التجارة) at Cairo University was founded in 1911 as the
*Higher School of Commerce*, and became the Faculty of Commerce when the higher schools were
merged into the Egyptian University (Cairo University) in 1935. It grants the bachelor's
degree over **four academic years**, and runs both a **regular system** (2 semesters/year)
and a **credit-hour system**.

The faculty's undergraduate departments include:

- **Accounting** (قسم المحاسبة)
- **Business Administration** (قسم إدارة الأعمال)
- **Mathematics & Insurance / Actuarial** (قسم الرياضة والتأمين)

Business Administration is the most flexible major and belongs to the **شعبة إدارة الأعمال**,
with the classic specializations:

- Production & Industrial Management (الإنتاج والإدارة الصناعية)
- Finance & Investment (التمويل والاستثمار)
- Marketing (التسويق)

Graduates receive the **bachelor's degree in commerce** in the chosen specialization.

## 2. Study System: Credit Hours, Load, Registration

- **Each course = 3 credit hours** unless marked otherwise. Some general university
  requirements are **2-credit** courses.
- The plan is distributed over **4 levels (years) × 2 semesters = 8 semesters**.
- A student normally registers **5–6 courses (15–20 credit hours)** per semester.
- **Maximum load** in a main semester: **21 credit hours** (allowed for students with a
  cumulative average ≥ 3.0, or a previous-semester average ≥ 3.0, or when needed to
  graduate).
- **Summer semester** load: normally **6 credit hours**; may rise to **9** with faculty
  council approval (e.g. for graduation reasons).
- At least **75% attendance** is required in courses with practical sessions; otherwise the
  student may be **barred from the exam** (حرمان) and recorded as failing.
- A course is passed with **≥ 50%** of the total mark.

  foundation courses.

## 5. Graduation Project

The graduation project is a two-part, year-long capstone:

- **BUS 401 — Graduation Project I (3 CH)** — Level 4, Semester 1: research proposal,
  literature review, and methodology.
- **BUS 402 — Graduation Project II (3 CH)** — Level 4, Semester 2: analysis, findings, and
  public defense (مناقشة).

It is assessed by a faculty committee through a written report and an oral defense.

## 7. Exam & Assessment System

Every course in every semester follows the faculty's continuous-assessment model, with a
**midterm** and a **final** exam.

### 7.1 Weighting

Faculty regulations set year-work at **no more than 30%** and the final exam at **at least
70%**. This plan uses the faculty's standard split:

| Component | Weight | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Year-work (أعمال السنة) | 20% | Assignments, quizzes, attendance, participation |
| Midterm (منتصف الفصل) | 20% | Held in Week 7 of each semester |
| **Final (نهاية الفصل)** | **60%** | 3-hour written exam, full syllabus |

*An alternative faculty-approved split is year-work 30% (midterm + attendance + practical
applications) and final 70%.*

### 7.2 Midterm exams (امتحانات منتصف الفصل)

- Held **once per semester, in Week 7**.
- Official windows: **first 3 weeks of November** (Fall) and **last third of March to first
  third of April** (Spring).
- Covers roughly the **first half** of each course.
- Typically 60–90 minutes: short answers, problems, and case questions.

### 7.3 Final exams (امتحانات نهاية الفصل)

- Held at the **end of each semester** (January for Fall; May–June for Spring).
- **3-hour written exam** covering the **full syllabus**.
- A **second round (دور ثانٍ)** is held for students who fail or miss with an accepted
  excuse.

### 7.4 Pass rules

- Pass mark = **50%** of the total mark.
- Students in courses with practical/tutorial sessions must have **≥ 75% attendance** or be
  barred from the exam.
- Borderline marks of **48 and 49 are raised to 50** automatically.
- **Mercy marks (درجات الرأفة)** may be applied within faculty limits for students near the
  pass line or at risk of dismissal.

## 9. Grading Scale & Academic Regulations

### 9.1 Grading bands (Egyptian system)

| Grade (AR) | Grade (EN) | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| ممتاز | Excellent | 90% and above |
| جيد جدًا | Very Good | 80% to < 90% |
| جيد | Good | 65% to < 80% |
| مقبول | Pass | 50% to < 65% |
| ضعيف | Weak (fail) | 35% to < 50% |
| ضعيف جدًا | Very Weak (fail) | Below 35% |

### 9.2 Progression rules

- A student moves to the next level after passing **all courses**, or after failing **no
  more than two courses** from their level or a lower level. Failed courses are re-sat with
  the previous level's students.
- **GPA:** a 4.0 scale is used in the credit-hour system; a cumulative average of at least
  **2.0** is normally required to graduate.
- **Mercy marks:** the faculty may raise borderline totals within set limits (e.g. up to
  ~2.5% of the year's total marks, capped per course), plus additional mercy for students at
  risk of final dismissal.
- **Attendance:** at least 75% attendance in practical courses; otherwise exam barring.

### 9.3 Graduation requirements

- Complete **144 credit hours** successfully.
- Pass the **Graduation Project** with a satisfactory grade and defend it.
- Achieve the minimum cumulative GPA.
- Settle all administrative and financial requirements.
